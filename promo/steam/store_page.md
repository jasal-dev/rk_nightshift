## About this game

**FIVE CASES. ONE NIGHT.**

It's after midnight in Los Angeles, and it's raining. You're Detective Ray Kessler, homicide, permanent night shift. Somebody has to stay up for them.

A piano player is dead behind the Blue Note. Before sunrise there will be four more bodies, five cases that look unrelated, and a reason for you to keep going back to where it started.

**NIGHTSHIFT is a point-and-click detective adventure in the old tradition: look at everything, talk to everyone, and think for yourself.** No hand-holding, no quest markers, no pixel hunting. Left click to walk and use, right click to look.

### The work

- **Read the scene.** Examine bodies, cars, phones, bar tabs and set lists. Every room is a pre-rendered 3D set, lit by neon, streetlamps and a single desk lamp.
- **Talk to the city.** Bartenders, rideshare drivers, a memorabilia dealer, a veteran in the back of a patrol car, the people who live and work along the night. Everyone has a version of the story. Some of them are true.
- **Keep your notes.** Everything you learn goes into your notebook, in your own words and in order.
- **Work the murder board.** Each case ends at the board. Pin the right cards in the right places and you close the case. Pin them wrong and you'll have to go back and think again. Pins carry over from case to case.
- **Four endings.** What you choose at the end of the night, and who you decide to trust with what you found, decides how the sun comes up.

### Your shift

- **12:15 AM, Dead Piano Player.** Behind the Blue Note, Hollywood. A man who played piano and owed money, and a bar that closes for the night too early.
- **1:40 AM, Five Stars.** A rideshare driver dead behind the wheel at the Mulholland overlook. Read his phone, go through his car, work out where he drove last.
- **2:45 AM, Walk of Fame.** A memorabilia dealer dead at the foot of his roof stairs on Hollywood Boulevard, and his Oscar gone. The roof stays dark until you find the breaker.
- **3:50 AM, Low Water.** A delivery rider dead in the LA River channel, a veteran from the camp cuffed in a patrol car, and half a broken headlight that has to be fitted back together.
- **4:50 AM, Last Call.** Back where the night began, and the one case you didn't see coming. Pier 9 at dawn.

### Features

- Five complete cases that run in one continuous night, from the first body to sunrise
- Hand-built 3D locations, pre-rendered at 1080p: a squad room, Hollywood Boulevard, the Mulholland overlook, a crowded diner, a Silver Lake apartment, a lit-up roof sign, a river channel, a crime lab, a pier at sunrise
- A voiced cast, with subtitles and adjustable text speed
- A notebook that writes itself, and a murder board that remembers
- Puzzles that use what you've found, from a UV lamp to a gate-camera clip to a smashed headlight you have to piece back together
- Four endings
- A moody soundtrack with a different track for each case
- Save and load whenever you like, with three save slots
- Windows. Mouse only; no controller support.

*Written, designed and built by one person. Rain included.*
