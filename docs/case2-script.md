# Nightshift – Case 2: Five Stars (Script)

Oct 4, 2026 · @Someone

## Overview

Case 2 runs from 1:40 to 2:45 a.m., about 25 minutes of play, and closes on the night: Devin Clark strangled his roommate Kenji Ota for a year of dashcam clips they sold to a gossip site. It also leaves the optional ride receipt, "Walt B.", Blue Note to Pryce Tower, on the murder board.

**Premise.** Kenji Ota, 29, a Glide rideshare driver, is found dead behind the wheel at the Mulholland overlook, engine running, wipers still going, the dashcam mount empty. The app says his night ended in Silver Lake at 11:52 p.m. His car says otherwise.

**Solution.** Kenji and Devin filmed celebrity passengers with a two-lens dashcam (road and back seat) and sold clips to HushHush, a gossip site. Three weeks ago a clip got a studio assistant, Carla Mendes, fired. Kenji decided to quit and give the cards to her lawyer. After his last fare he picked Devin up at home, they argued over pie at Norm's, and Kenji drove them up to the overlook where they used to talk. Devin, in the back seat, strangled him with his camera bag strap, unscrewed the dashcam, emptied the memory card case in the glovebox, locked it again and walked down Mulholland in the rain. The dashcam and eleven memory cards are in Devin's freezer, inside a bag of frozen peas.

**Flow.** Drive up (Otis's text) → Mulholland overlook (Officer Park, Dr. Shah, the Prius: phone app, car GPS, receipt, glovebox) → Norm's diner and Kenji's apartment in either order (Rosa and the card slip; Heck's alibi; Devin's lies, the freezer) → drive back (Maya's text) → squad room (deduction, Doyle's call, Otis rings with Case 3).

The case is the first with a choice of destinations. Norm's and the apartment can be visited in either order, but Devin's story only breaks once Ray has proof from Norm's and the car's GPS.

## Script

Five scenes. Scene 1 is a transition, Scenes 3 and 4 can be played in either order, and Scene 5 returns to the squad room.

### Scene 1. The drive up (1:40 a.m.)

A non-interactive transition, about 20 seconds, skippable. It opens on the title card from the end of Case 1.

*Rain on the windshield. The car climbs the Cahuenga Pass, then turns onto the dark curves of Mulholland Drive. The city's lights swing in and out of view.*

RAY (V.O.): Mulholland Drive. Twenty miles of curves along the top of the hills, so the rich can look down on the rest of us.

*Ray's phone buzzes on the dashboard. A text bubble appears.*

OTIS (text): overlook past the Bowl turnoff. patrol is Officer Park. she's new. be nice

RAY (typing): I'm always nice.

OTIS (text): be nicer

RAY (V.O.): Up here the rain comes in sideways. Down there, four million people are asleep, or wish they were.

*Cut to the overlook.*

### Scene 2. The Mulholland overlook (1:55 a.m.)

Goal: work the scene and learn that Kenji's night went on after the app said it ended. Introduces Dr. Shah and the device screens (the Glide app and the car's GPS).

EXT. MULHOLLAND SCENIC OVERLOOK. NIGHT. WIND AND RAIN.

*A dirt pullout above the whole lit-up city, a low stone wall at the edge. A white Prius sits nose to the view, engine idling, headlights on, wipers sweeping every few seconds. Yellow tape runs between two patrol cones. OFFICER LENA PARK, rain cape, holds the tape. DR. ANITA SHAH crouches at the open driver's door with a flashlight. KENJI OTA is slumped behind the wheel, face turned toward the window. A coroner's van idles at the right. Ray's car at the left.*

RAY: The overlook. Best view in Los Angeles. He had it all to himself, and he isn't looking.

PARK: Detective Kessler? Officer Park. Lena. I've held the scene since one twenty-two. Nobody's touched the car but Dr. Shah.

RAY: Who found him?

PARK: A couple came up to, uh, look at the view. Engine running, wipers going, nobody moving. I took their statement and let them go home.

**Talking to Park.**

[choice] What have we got? / Who was his last passenger? / Anything else from the couple? (after the first two) / That'll do.

*What have we got?*

RAY: Wallet left on the body. I've seen that one this week.

*Who was his last passenger?*

*Park holds out her phone. The phone panel shows a shaky selfie video from a back seat.*
