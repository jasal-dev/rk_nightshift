## 1. Introduction

`[INT]`

Los Angeles, the present day, a Thursday night turning into Friday, and raining. Detective Ray Kessler of Hollywood
Homicide, Room 214, has one shift, from a quarter past midnight to sunrise. Danny Reyes, a piano player at the Blue
Note jazz club across the street, was shot in the alley two nights ago, and the lieutenant wants it closed by Friday.

The night is five cases. Case 1 starts on Danny and stalls. Cases 2, 3 and 4 are new calls from the front desk, each one
solved on its own, and each one hides a small piece of paper that matters later. Case 5 brings everything back to
the Blue Note, and ends at Pier 9 at dawn with a choice of who gets the evidence.

| Case | Title | Rough time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dead Piano Player | 35 min |
| 2 | Five Stars | 25 min |
| 3 | Walk of Fame | 30 min |
| 4 | Low Water | 30 min |
| 5 | Last Call | 45 min |

## 2. Controls

`[CTL]`

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left click** | Walk, or do the hotspot's action (Talk, Go, Drive, Take, Open, Knock, Use, Search...). |
| **Right click** | Look at a hotspot. In the inventory, examine or read an item. |
| Mouse at the top edge, or **Tab** | Show the inventory bar (Tab pins it open). |
| Click an item, then a hotspot | Use the item on it. Right click or **Esc** puts the item away. |
| Click the **notebook** | Opens it full screen (left or right click). |
| Click, **Space** | Skip the current line of dialogue. |
| **1 to 9** | Pick a dialogue option. |
| **F5 / F9** | Save / load menu: three slots, each with a screenshot, the case and the date. |
| **Esc** | Save menu (with EXIT WITHOUT SAVING), then back to the title screen (when nothing else is open). |
| **F11** or **Alt+Enter** | Toggle fullscreen. |

**The cursor tells you what each button does.** Over a hotspot it shows the hotspot's name above, the left-click action
at the lower left and "Look" at the lower right. Some things are look-only: left click looks at them and right click
does nothing.

**The notebook** opens as a paper book with one page per case, open at the current case. Notes you haven't read yet are
highlighted with a red NEW. The arrows in the corners turn back to earlier cases. Close it with the Close button, Esc,
a right click, or a click outside the book (Esc here only closes the book, it doesn't save and quit).

**Title screen.** New Game, Load Game (three slots), Settings (fullscreen and text advance: Auto, where lines move on by themselves and a click skips, or
Manual, the default, where every line waits for a click) and Exit. The "SKIP TO" row starts Case 2, 3, 4 or 5 directly with every
earlier case solved, including all three optional seeds.

## 3. How the game works

`[HOW]`

- **Talking.** Conversations offer a list of topics. Topics you've asked drop off the list, and new ones appear as you
  learn things. When someone has nothing left for you, Ray says so instead of opening the menu.
- **Clues.** Facts go into the notebook automatically ("Notebook updated"). Each case's deduction only uses that case's
  notes and items.
- **Breaking a lie.** Suspects in Cases 2 to 5 tell a story that falls apart in two or three steps. You either pick
  the new dialogue option that appears once you have the proof, or use the proof item on them.
- **The murder board** in Room 214 is where each case ends. First you name the culprit (a wrong name just gets a line
  from Ray, pick again), then you pin evidence into labelled slots. A wrong card gets a line and comes back down.
  After three wrong rounds Ray gives you a hint. Nothing is lost by guessing, and "(Step back from the board)" always
  gets you out.
- **Hints.** If you wander around a room without making progress, Ray will eventually mutter a nudge.
- **Items don't follow you between cases.** At the start of each new case, the previous case's items stay on the desk.
  The notebook comes along.
- **Seeds.** Three optional pieces of paper, one each in Cases 2, 3 and 4, get pinned next to Danny on the board. You
  need all three for the best ending. See [The three seeds](#5-the-three-seeds-optional).

### Case 1: Dead Piano Player

`[C1]` · 12:15 a.m. · Room 214, the street, Pier 9

**Cast:** Ray Kessler; Lt. Maureen Doyle (on the phone); Nina Alvarez, Danny's girlfriend (on the phone); Sal Moretti,
the Blue Note's bartender (through the door); Tiny Ruiz, the doorman at Pier 9; Vance, a loan shark.

#### `[C1.1]` Room 214, the squad room

1. **Look** at the desk lamp: "The base sits a little crooked." Then **use** the lamp to get the **small key** taped under
   it.
2. Use the **small key** on the **filing cabinet** to get the **Reyes file**.
3. Open the inventory and **right click the Reyes file** to read it. This writes the first two notes: shot once with a
   .38 at close range, and wallet left on the body but phone never found.
4. **Take** the **mug** to get the **cold coffee**. Use the **cold coffee** on the **wastebasket** to pour it out and find a
   **dime** at the bottom.
5. **(optional)** Use the **desk phone** to hear Lt. Doyle's voicemail.
6. Go out the **door** at the back. You can't leave without the Reyes file.

#### `[C1.2]` The street outside the Blue Note

1. **(optional)** Look at the rezoning notice on the wall: Pryce Development wants the block.
2. Use the **dumpster** at the mouth of the alley to find a **matchbook** from the Blue Note. There's a number written inside:
   555-0147.
3. Use the **dime** on the **payphone**. Nina answers. Pick:
   - "I'm calling about Danny Reyes."
   - "Who am I talking to?" (optional, but Nina remembers you for it in Case 5)
   - "How do I get Sal to open the door?" She gives you **Danny's knock: two slow, three fast.**

   If you hang up without the knock, the phone gives the dime back, so you can't get stuck.
4. **Knock** on the **Blue Note door**. Sal answers to Danny's knock. Ask "Did you see who shot him?" (optional), then
   "Who did Danny owe money to?" Sal names **Vance, Pier 9, the green door**.
5. Use **your car** to drive south to San Pedro.

#### `[C1.3]` Pier 9

1. Tiny won't let you in, and talking to him or flashing the badge gets nowhere. Use the **matchbook** on **Tiny**. He
   drinks at the Blue Note, and he liked Danny.
2. **Knock** on the **green door**. Danny's knock gets you in: Danny came here every Tuesday to pay.

#### `[C1.4]` Vance's office

1. **(optional)** Look at the **.45** on the desk, and use the **Reyes file** on **Vance** (he points you at his wall).
2. **Use** the **wall of IOUs** (markers): Danny's $8,000 marker is stamped PAID, dated Tuesday.
3. Talk to **Vance**:
   - "Danny's marker says PAID." Danny paid in full, in cash, Tuesday afternoon, and said there was more money coming.
   - "How did he pay you?" In a Blue Note envelope with something written on the back. Tiny burns the trash every night.
   - "Anyone else asking about Danny?" **(optional, recommended)** A man who "talked like a cop who'd stopped being one"
     called on Wednesday.
   - "Where were you Tuesday night?" **(optional)** He was on his boat in Avalon.
   - "I'll be going." Back to the pier.

#### `[C1.5]` Pier 9, the burn barrel

1. **Take** the **boat hook** from the **piling**.
2. Use the **boat hook** on the **burn barrel** to fish out the **Blue Note envelope**. On the back, in Danny's hand:
   **"1 of 3"**. Ray hangs the hook back on its nail.
3. Use **your car** to drive back to Room 214.

#### `[C1.6]` Room 214, the murder board

1. Use the **murder board** (or the **envelope** on it). The question: "Why?"
   - Answer: **"For something he had."**
2. Pin the evidence. Correct cards: **Envelope, "1 of 3"** and **Wallet left, phone gone**.
3. The case stays open. Use the **desk phone** to call Lt. Doyle. Listen closely during the call: there's a muffled
   aside in her office ("Thanks, Walt. Black is fine."). Then Otis at the front desk calls with the next case.

### Case 2: Five Stars

`[C2]` · 1:40 a.m. · The Mulholland overlook, Kenji's car, Norm's on Sunset, Kenji's apartment in Silver Lake

**Cast:** Kenji Ota, a Glide rideshare driver (the victim); Officer Lena Park; Dr. Anita Shah, the coroner's investigator;
Rosa, a waitress at Norm's; Heck Dominguez, a rival driver; Devin Clark, Kenji's roommate.

#### `[C2.1]` The Mulholland overlook

1. Talk to **Officer Park**: "What have we got?", "Who was his last passenger?" (the passenger's video: Kenji had a
   camera pointed at the back seat, and the passenger has 31,000 witnesses for an alibi), then "Anything else from the
   couple?" (a man was walking down Mulholland at 1:15 with a bag, and no car).
2. Talk to **Dr. Shah**. She makes you put on **gloves**. Nothing in the car can be touched without them. Ask "How did he
   die?" (strangled from the back seat with an inch-wide herringbone strap), "When?" (1:00 to 1:15) and
   "Anything else?" (cherry pie crust on his shirt).
3. **(optional)** Look at the Prius, then look at and use the **scratch**: RAT was keyed into the door weeks ago.
   Look at the **ground**: pale orange grit, the decomposed granite of the parking lot. You'll want this before
   Kenji's apartment so Ray recognizes it on a pair of shoes.
4. Use the **open driver's door** to lean into the car.

#### `[C2.2]` Inside Kenji's car

1. Use the **phone in the dash mount** (the Glide app):
   - **Trips** tab: his last fare ended in Silver Lake at 11:52. After that the app says he went home.
   - **(optional, seed)** Still in Trips, tap **"Earlier this week >"**, then **"More v"** twice to reach Wednesday. Tap
     **"Wed 1:10 AM  Blue Note, Hollywood > Pryce Tower, Century City  -  Walt B.  *1"**. Ray photographs the screen:
     the **photo of Kenji's screen** (the ride receipt, billed to Pryce Development). This is the first of the three seeds.
   - **Messages** tab: Heck's threat from Tuesday. Close the app.
2. Use the **dashboard screen** (the head unit): **Navigation**, then **Recent destinations**. Home at 11:56 for two minutes,
   Norm's at 12:08, the overlook at 12:58. Ray works out that Kenji drove off the app with someone he knew.
3. Use the **ignition** to switch off the engine and take **Kenji's keys**. (It refuses until you've read the dashboard
   screen.)
4. **(optional)** Use **Kenji's keys** on the **glovebox**: an empty case for twelve memory cards, January to December.
5. Use the **door pocket** to take **Norm's receipt** (table 6, 12:14 a.m.).
6. Use the **sun visor** to take **Kenji's driver card**.
7. **(optional)** Use the **dashcam mount** and the **cups**, and look at the back seat.
8. Use **Out** to climb back out, then use **your car**. Park takes Kenji's keys. Pick **"Norm's on Sunset"**. (If you skipped the glovebox, ask Park
   "Can I have Kenji's keys back?" when you come back up; she takes them again when you drive off.)

You can't leave until you've had Park's answer about the passenger and worked out the off-app trip. Norm's and the
apartment can be visited in either order, but Norm's first makes the apartment easier.

#### `[C2.3]` Norm's on Sunset

1. Rosa is too busy to talk. Use the **coffee pot** on the warmer: Ray pours for the regulars at the counter.
2. Talk to **Rosa**: "I'm looking for a Glide driver. Kenji Ota." She knows faces, not names.
3. Use **Kenji's driver card** on **Rosa**. Kenji was in booth 6 with his roommate, "the one with the camera bag". They
   argued about "cards".
4. Talk to **Rosa**: "Who paid?" (the roommate, on a card), "When did they leave?", then "Can I see the card slip?" With
   Norm's receipt in your pocket she finds it: **Norm's card slip**, signed "D. Clark". (You can also use the receipt on
   her.)
5. **(optional)** Talk to **Heck** in the back booth: "Kenji Ota.", "\"I'll put you in the ground.\"" (his alibi: the LAX
   queue all night), then "Was Kenji into anything?" (Kenji was quitting "the side thing") and "What about his roommate?".
6. **(optional)** Use **booth 6**: a napkin that says "11 × 2,500" with "50/50" crossed out.
7. Use the **door**, and pick **"Kenji's place, Silver Lake"**.

#### `[C2.4]` Kenji's apartment

1. Talk to **Devin**: "Who are you?", **"When did you last see Kenji?"** (home all night, asleep by eleven), "Did Kenji
   have enemies?", "Mind if I look around?".
2. Look around while he leans on the fridge (all of these are worth it):
   - Look at the **camera bag**: an inch-wide herringbone strap.
   - Look at the **sneakers**: soaked, with orange grit from the overlook (Ray only recognizes the grit if you looked at
     the overlook's ground).
   - Look at the **laptop**: "editing all night", but the last save was 6:12 p.m.
   - Use **Kenji's desk**: a note. Kenji was giving the memory cards to a fired studio assistant's lawyer.
3. **First lie.** Pick **"You weren't home all night."** (or use the **card slip** on Devin). He admits Norm's, and says
   Kenji dropped him back at 12:40.
4. **Second lie.** Pick **"His car never came back here."** The car's GPS says it never did. Devin tells you to search
   the place, and moves to the couch.
5. **(optional)** Look at the **fridge**: Kenji's note about Devin's peas, untouched since July.
6. Use the **freezer**: a **bag of frozen peas**, taped shut.
7. Use the **bag of frozen peas** on the **sink** (or right click it in the inventory). Inside: a **dashcam and eleven
   memory cards**. Devin asks for a lawyer and Park comes up the stairs.
8. Use the **front door** and pick **"Room 214"**.

#### `[C2.5]` Room 214, the murder board

1. **(seed)** If you have the **photo of Kenji's screen**, you don't need to do anything: it gets pinned beside Danny
   automatically when the case closes. Using it on the board now just gets "After Kenji."
2. Use the **murder board**. The question: "Who?"
   - Answer: **"Devin Clark, his roommate."**
3. Pin the evidence:
   - **He was with Kenji tonight:** **Norm's card slip, "D. Clark", 12:14** (or **Rosa: the roommate with the camera bag**).
   - **What he killed him for:** **Dashcam and memory cards, in the peas**.
4. Doyle calls, then Otis with the next case.

### Case 3: Walk of Fame

`[C3]` · 2:45 a.m. · Stardust Memorabilia on Hollywood Boulevard: the shop, the back office, the roof

**Cast:** Gus Lindqvist, 71, the owner (the victim); Pearl Danvers, his assistant; Morty Kahn, a rival collector from
three doors down; Officer Park; Dr. Shah; Charlie, a Charlie Chaplin impersonator on the next fire escape.

#### `[C3.1]` The shop

1. Look at the **front door** (right click; going out is "Not yet" until you have): the glass is out on the sidewalk, so it was broken from the inside.
2. Use the **display case**: the Oscar's case was opened with a key. Ray concludes the robbery was staged.
3. **(optional)** Use the **register**: $212 left in it.
4. Talk to **Pearl**: **"What happened tonight?"** (she closed at nine, came back at 2:15 for her headshots, and found Gus),
   "How long did you work for Gus?", "Who has keys to the Oscar case?" (only Gus and Pearl), "Who'd want the Oscar?".
5. Talk to **Park**: "Who's the man in the bathrobe?", "Anything from the canvass?" (Charlie is on his fire escape).
6. Talk to **Morty**: "Who are you?", "You wanted the Oscar.", "Where were you tonight?" (an online auction in Tokyo, from
   12:30 to 2:10).
7. Go through the **curtain** to the back office.

#### `[C3.2]` The back office

1. Talk to **Dr. Shah**: "How did he die?" (she hands you **Gus's keys**), "When?" (1:15 to 1:45), **"Did he fall, or was he
   helped?"** (two small handprints on his chest: he was pushed), "Anything else?" (something small and star-shaped
   scratched his palm).
2. Use the **desk**: Pryce Development's eviction letter.
3. Use the **desk** again: **Calloway's catalogue**. Gus found his own Oscar, serial No. 734, listed as sold three weeks ago.
4. **(optional, seed)** Use the **desk** (or the **letter**) a third time: under the letter is **Walter Brenner's card**,
   "LAPD (Ret.), Security Consultant, Pryce Development". This is the second of the three seeds.
5. Use **Gus's keys** on the **drawer**: a **UV lamp**, with Gus's note "Modern ink glows. Old ink doesn't."
6. Use the **cordless phone**, Calls tab, and tap the 12:52 call: Gus called Calloway's at 12:40 and **Pearl at 12:52**. She left
   that out.
7. Use **Pearl's locker** twice: her headshots are still there (so she didn't come back for them), then a legal pad with
   "Lyle Brandt" practised forty times in gold pen.
8. Use the **fuse panel**: the roof sign was on a timer that turned it off at one. Switch it back on. (You can look at
   the panel first for the labels. Without the sign, the roof is pitch dark and Ray won't walk around on it.)

#### `[C3.3]` Back in the shop

1. Use the **UV lamp** on the **photo wall**: three "vintage" signatures glow, so they're modern gold paint pen.
2. Use **Calloway's catalogue** on the **certificate**: the same serial number, No. 734.
3. Talk to **Morty**: "Calloway's sold No. 734." He bought the real Oscar for $180,000 from "Stardust Archive", and Pearl
   runs the shop's email. Then **"What does a real one weigh?"**: eight and a half pounds of metal. A prop is hollow resin
   and floats.
4. **(optional)** Talk to **Park**: "The developer's man." She has already cleared Pryce's agent on camera.

#### `[C3.4]` The office, then the roof

1. Back in the office, use the **UV lamp** on **Pearl's locker**: her autograph on the headshots glows the same gold. Same
   pen, same hand.
2. Take the **stairs** to the lit roof.
3. Use the **gravel**: small heel prints from the door to the water tank and back, and none to the edge.
4. **(optional)** Look at the **lawn chair** (Gus's cigar, half smoked: he came up to wait for someone).
5. **Take** the **glint** at the top of the stairs: a **gold star earring**, torn out of somebody's ear.
6. Use the **water tank**: the "Oscar" floats. It's a hollow resin prop with No. 734 freshly engraved. You get **the "Oscar"**.
7. Talk to **Charlie**: "What did you see tonight?" (Pearl's yellow Fiat in the alley around one), "Did you hear
   anything?" (Gus shouting "How long?" around 1:30), "You knew Gus?". **(optional)** If you have Brenner's card:
   **"Do you know this man? Walter Brenner."** He went door to door last Thursday and said the block would be empty by Christmas, "the
   jazz club too".
8. Go back down through the **roof door**, then through the **curtain** to the shop.

#### `[C3.5]` Breaking Pearl

1. Talk to **Pearl**: **"Gus called you at twelve fifty-two."** (or "Your headshots are still in your locker."). She admits
   she came in at one.
2. Use **the "Oscar"** on **Pearl** (or pick "Lyle was in the water tank."). She admits selling the real one, and says Gus
   had already fallen.
3. Use the **gold star earring** on **Pearl** (or pick "Gus didn't fall."). She confesses, and Park takes her out.
4. **(optional)** Talk to **Morty** once more. It changes one end card.
5. Use the **front door** to drive back to Room 214.

#### `[C3.6]` Room 214, the murder board

1. **(seed)** Brenner's card pins itself beside Danny when the case closes, if you took it.
2. Use the **murder board**. The question: "Whose?"
   - Answer: **"Pearl Danvers, his assistant."**
3. Pin the evidence:
   - **She was selling him out:** **Legal pad: "Lyle Brandt" forty times** (or **Headshots in the same gold pen**, or
     **Morty paid "Stardust Archive"; Pearl runs the email**).
   - **She was at the top of his stairs:** **Gold star earring, top of the stairs**.
4. Doyle calls, then Otis with the next case. Preacher, Otis warns, will only talk to "somebody who's been somewhere".

### Case 4: Low Water

`[C4]` · 3:50 a.m. · The Fletcher Drive bridge, the LA River channel, a glass house in Los Feliz, a garage in Mount
Washington, the crime lab

**Cast:** Owen Tate, 24, a Chomp delivery rider (the victim); Officer Brian Doss; Calvin "Preacher" Odom, a veteran from the
camp by the bridge; Dr. Shah; Courtney Vail, an event planner; Andre, the valet; Elliot Crane, a councilman's chief of
staff; Ike Feld at the lab.

#### `[C4.1]` The Fletcher Drive bridge

1. **(optional)** Talk to **Doss**: "What have you got?", "Who found him?", "Why's he in your car?". "Uncuff him." won't work yet.
2. Talk to **Preacher** in the patrol car. He only talks to somebody who's been somewhere:
   - **"I've been somewhere."**
   - **"Baghdad, oh-three. Military Police."**
   - **"Assist, Protect, Defend."** (the MP motto; wrong answers just start over)

   Then ask everything: "What happened tonight?" (he found the bike on the bridge and carried it down so it wouldn't be
   stripped), "Did you hear anything?" (a bang around two, no brakes), "Did you see the boy?", "Why carry the bike all
   the way down?".
3. Look at the **road**: no skid marks. Use the **gutter**: headlight plastic at the east end. Use the **storm drain**:
   the bridge drains into the channel.
4. Take the **stairs** down to the channel.

#### `[C4.2]` The river channel

1. Talk to **Dr. Shah**: "How did he die?" (hit by a car from behind), "When?" (1:45 to 2:30), **"The scrapes on him."** (they
   barely bled: he was dead when somebody slid him down the bank), "Anything else?" (headlight plastic in his hair).
2. Use the **bike** twice: blue-gray paint and a **sliver of headlight**, then a nursing textbook.
3. Look at Owen's **phone** on the bike (a $900 phone Preacher never touched), then use it: the lock screen shows his last
   drop-off on **Glendower Avenue** at 1:50, a one-star review ("Threatened that someone would get killed"), and the trip
   paused at 2:04 on Fletcher Drive.
4. Look at the **east bank** (heel drag marks from the fence gap) and look at the **stairs** (one tire line and boot prints).
   Two sets of tracks: the bike came down with Preacher, Owen came down the bank with somebody else.
5. Try the **reeds** under the bridge. It's too dark. Remember that.
6. Go back up the **stairs**.

#### `[C4.3]` The bridge again

1. Talk to **Doss**: **"Uncuff him."** With Shah's findings and the two sets of tracks (or the untouched phone), Doss lets
   Preacher go.
2. Talk to **Doss** again: **"Put your spotlight on the reeds."**
3. Take the **stairs** down.

#### `[C4.4]` The channel, lit

1. **(optional)** Talk to **Preacher** at his tent: "Where does stuff end up, off that bridge?" (the reeds under the drain),
   "Will you be all right?".
2. Use the **reeds**: **half a headlight** (four rings, part 4K0: an Audi), and deeper in the mud, a phone in a cracked black
   case with a Blue Note sticker. It's **Danny's phone**, two nights in the river.
3. **(optional, recommended)** Use **Danny's phone** on **Preacher** (or ask "Ever seen this?"). Tuesday after three, a big man in
   an old cop's coat threw something small and black off the bridge from a long gray car.
4. Go up the **stairs** and use **your car**: **"Glendower Avenue, Los Feliz"**.

#### `[C4.5]` The glass house

1. Talk to **Courtney**: "Whose party was it?" (Harlan Pryce's, for Councilmember Haskell), "You ordered from Chomp
   tonight.", **"You reported the rider."**, then **"Tell her about Owen."** She opens the gate camera app. Watch all five
   clips:
   - 1:49 AM, front gate: Owen arrives late in the rain.
   - **1:51 AM, driveway:** a drunk guest takes his own keys off the valet board. Owen tells him he can't drive like that,
     and gets "Mind your business, delivery boy."
   - 1:53 AM, front gate: Owen rides off downhill.
   - **1:55 AM, front gate:** a blue-gray Audi follows him, both headlights intact. **Plate 8KXD392.**
   - 2:38 AM, front gate: Courtney locks up from inside. She never left.
2. Talk to **Andre**: "Who left drunk tonight?", then **"I need the ticket."** You get **valet ticket No. 47**: "Crane. Out
   1:55. Guest insisted." **(optional)** "Who's the last car?" and "Who left early?" (Pryce left at eleven in a gray
   Lincoln, driven by "a big old ex-cop").
3. **(optional, seed)** Look at the **box of gift bags**, then **take** from it: **Pryce's invitation**. This is the third
   seed, and the easiest to miss. Right click it in the inventory to read it: the drawing puts a skyscraper lobby where the
   Blue Note is.

### Case 5: Last Call

`[C5]` · 4:50 a.m. · The street (crime scene), the Blue Note bar and back room, Room 214, the lab, Pier 9 at dawn

**Cast:** Sal Moretti (the victim); Mara Quist, an LA Times reporter; Teo, the porter who found him; Officer Park; Dr. Shah;
Nina Alvarez; Otis; Lt. Doyle; Ike Feld; Vance and Tiny; and a big man in an old raincoat.

You start with **your phone** in the inventory. Click it (left or right) to use it (texts and calls).

#### `[C5.1]` The street

1. Head for the **Blue Note door**. **Mara Quist** of the Times stops you. Any answer works; she gives you **Mara Quist's card**.
2. **(optional)** Talk to **Mara**: "What are you working on?", "What do you know about the block?" (Hollywood Core, forty
   stories, and Haskell is the swing vote).
3. Talk to **Park**: "What happened?", "Who cut him down?", "What's patrol calling it?" (no note, from a man who wrote down
   every drink for thirty years). **(optional, recommended)** "Danny's alley, Tuesday.": a retired cop named Walt stood
   inside Danny's tape for an hour on Tuesday night and asked whether they'd found the phone.
4. Talk to **Teo**: "You found him?", "The front door." (on the latch, not the deadbolt: somebody left and pulled it shut),
   "Sal's habits." ("If it isn't in the book, it didn't happen."), "Who knows the knock?" (since Tuesday Sal only opened to
   Danny's knock).
5. Go in through the **Blue Note door**.

#### `[C5.2]` The bar (first visit)

1. Take **Sal's tab book** from beside the register. Use the **register**: the till is empty and nothing's taken. Not a robbery.
2. **Right click the tab book** in the inventory to read its three bookmarked pages:
   - Tonight: "12:30 KESSLER, THRU DOOR" and **"3:20 W.B. CLUB SODA. N/C."**, after closing.
   - Tuesday: a private booking in the back booth, three men, two Macallan 18s and a club soda, cash, no names.
   - The margin: **"D.R. DECAF"**, every night.
3. Look at the **drying rack**: one glass washed tonight.
4. **(optional)** Look at the **piano**: letters taped on the white keys, C D E F G A B.
5. **Take** the **set list** from the piano's music stand: Tuesday's set, and under "AFTER: for Sal", five notes on a short staff.
6. Go through the **back door** at the end of the bar.

#### `[C5.3]` The back room

1. Talk to **Dr. Shah**: "How did he die?" (dead before the rope: a bar-arm choke from behind, a hold the LAPD banned in
   1982), "When?" (3:30 to 4:00), "Did he fight?" (no: he turned his back on his killer), "Anything else?".
2. Look at the **stool** (four feet from the pipe), use **Sal** (no rope burn on his palms), use **Danny's locker** (Sal's keys in
   it, the sheet music searched) and use the **desk** (Pryce's buyout offer, with NO written across it). Ray concludes the
   hanging was staged.
3. **(optional)** Look at the **alley door** (deadbolted from inside) and the **hooks** twice (a dripping red umbrella, then small
   wet sneaker prints leading to the cooler).
4. Use the **walk-in cooler**. Somebody's holding the handle from inside. Don't use the knock. Pick **"Nina? It's Ray
   Kessler."** If you didn't get Nina's name on the phone in Case 1, she asks how she knows you: pick **"I called you from
   the payphone across the street."**
5. Talk to **Nina**:
   - "What happened tonight?" (she heard a man ask "What did the kid leave you, Sal?" at 3:30)
   - "The man who called you." (he called her "young lady", from a police number)
   - **"Can I see your phone?"**: an incoming call at **1:52 a.m. from (323) 555-0186**, two minutes. **Required.**
   - "What did Danny have?", "Where does the knock come from?" (a five-note tune Danny wrote for Sal), **"Did Danny record his
     sets?"** (on his phone, every set), "Stay with Dr. Shah."
6. Back through the bar and out the **front door**, then the **precinct door**. (The precinct is closed to you until you've
   seen Nina's phone.)

#### `[C5.4]` Room 214: the leak

1. Use the **desk phone**: **Call log**. One call out from your desk all night: 1:30 a.m., to Lt. Doyle's office.
2. Use the **phone list** on the wall: Doyle's direct line is **(323) 555-0186**. Nina's caller used the lieutenant's phone.
3. Use the **murder board**. Ray replays the 1:30 call in his head. Pick the line that matters: **"Thanks, Walt. Black is fine."**

4. Use the **desk phone**: **Call Otis**. His visitor log: **Walter Brenner**, retired, in at 1:20, out at 2:05, brought coffee.

## 6. The four endings

`[END]`

All four start from the same sunrise. Before choosing, it's worth a quick save (F5) so you can see them all.

**Ending A: Doyle, the best ending.** Pick "Call Lt. Doyle." with all three seeds pinned. Doyle comes to the pier and argues
against the case three times. Answer each by picking the matching card from Danny's box:

1. "That doesn't make him Pryce's man." → **Walter Brenner's card**
2. "You can't put Walt with Pryce the night Reyes died." → **Photo of Walt B.'s ride, billed to Pryce Development**
3. "Haskell... his word against a dead piano player's phone." → **Pryce's invitation**

A wrong card just gets her rebuttal, and you pick again. Doyle takes the drive by the book, every comma. Pryce, Haskell and
Brenner all fall.

**Ending B: Doyle, the bitter ending.** Pick "Call Lt. Doyle." with one or more seeds missing. When an objection comes up that
you have no card for, the only way out is "I don't have it." Doyle takes the drive anyway. Brenner goes down for Sal; Danny's
case is closed as a robbery, the recording goes nowhere, and Pryce gets his tower.

**Ending C: Internal Affairs and the DA.** Pick "Call Internal Affairs and the DA." Ray reports the leak from the lieutenant's
office and hands the drive to a deputy DA from Public Integrity. Slow and legal: Brenner is charged with both murders, Haskell
resigns, and Pryce's lawyers are still filing motions.

**Ending D: The Times.** Pick "Call Mara Quist at the Times." The story runs at noon and the audio goes everywhere. Hollywood
Core is withdrawn and Brenner is charged, but Pryce is never charged, and Ray is suspended for sixty days for releasing
evidence to the press.

**End card variations:**

- How you ended the standoff on the pier (talked down or forced) changes one end card.
- Talking to Morty after Pearl's arrest in Case 3 adds one about Gus and the real Oscar.

## 9. Tips (spoiler-free)

`[TIP]`

- **Look at everything.** A right click costs nothing, and some clues only come from looking, not using.
- **Watch the cursor.** It names the hotspot and tells you exactly what each mouse button will do there.
- **Exhaust every conversation.** New topics appear as you learn things, so come back to people.
- **When a door won't open, Ray tells you why.** "Not yet" usually means there's one more person to talk to or one more thing
  to look at in this location.
- **Read your items.** Some papers say more than their names suggest. Right click them in the inventory.
- **Read the notebook** when you're stuck. The NEW notes show what you learned most recently.
- **The board is forgiving.** Wrong guesses cost nothing. If you can't decide, try a card and listen to Ray's line.
- **Stand still and Ray will help.** Wander for a while without progress and he'll think out loud.
- **Some pieces of paper matter later.** If something in a case seems unrelated to that case, take it anyway.
- **Save before big moments** with F5, especially at the end of the night. Esc opens the save menu before it goes back to the title.
- **Prefer to read at your own pace?** Manual is the default text advance: each line waits for a click. Auto in Settings moves on by itself.
