# Nightshift — Case 4: Low Water (Script)

Oct 4, 2026 · @Someone

## Overview

Case 4 runs from 3:50 to 4:50 a.m., about 30 minutes of play, and closes on the night: Elliot Crane, chief of staff to Councilman Ted Haskell, ran down a delivery rider on the Fletcher Drive bridge while driving home drunk, then slid the body into the river channel so it would look like the camp did it. On the way, Ray pulls Danny Reyes's phone out of the reeds, and the optional seed is the invitation to Harlan Pryce's fundraiser for Haskell.

**Premise.** Owen Tate, 24, a rider for the delivery app Chomp, is found on the concrete apron of the LA River under the Fletcher Drive bridge in Elysian Valley. A Metro bus driver saw his yellow delivery bag in the headlights at 3:22. Northeast Division uniforms found Owen's e-bike at the tent of an Army veteran the camp calls Preacher and cuffed him. Northeast has no homicide detective on nights, so the call goes to Ray.

**Solution.** Harlan Pryce hosted a fundraiser for Councilman Haskell tonight at a glass house above Los Feliz. Owen's last delivery was there, staff dinner for the event planner, forty minutes late. At the gate he saw Crane, drunk, take his own keys off the valet board, and told him he shouldn't drive. Crane told him to mind his business. Owen rode off at 1:53; Crane left at 1:55, the same way home. At about 2:04, on the bridge, the Audi hit Owen from behind without braking. Crane stopped at the end of the bridge, saw tents below, dragged Owen through a gap in the fence and slid him down the bank toward the camp. He left the bike against the rail and drove home to Mount Washington, where he has been washing the car under a new tarp ever since. Most of the broken headlight went down the bridge's storm drain into the reeds, where it lies beside a phone someone threw off the same bridge two nights ago.

**Flow.** Drive in (Shah's text) → Fletcher Drive bridge (Doss, Preacher in the patrol car, glass in the gutter, the storm drain) → the channel (Shah and Owen, the bike at Preacher's tent, Owen's phone, two sets of tracks, then the spotlight and the reeds: the headlight and Danny's phone) → the glass house in Los Feliz (Courtney Vail, the gate camera, Andre the valet and his ticket, the gift bags) → Otis runs the plate → Crane's garage in Mount Washington (the tarp, the loafers, three lies, the headlight fits) → the night lab (Danny's phone to Ike) → squad room (deduction, pins, Doyle's call, Otis rings with Case 5).

The bridge and the channel connect on foot. After that the car menu offers the glass house and, once the plate is run, Crane's house, in either order, though Crane's lies only break with evidence from the house. Freeing Preacher is the first gate; the spotlight on the reeds is the second.

## Script

Seven scenes. Scene 1 is a transition; Scenes 2 and 3 are connected on foot; Scenes 4 and 5 are reached by the car menu; Scene 6 is a short stop at the lab on the way back; Scene 7 returns to the squad room.

### Scene 1. Riverside Drive (3:55 a.m.)

A non-interactive transition, about 20 seconds, skippable. It opens on the title card from the end of Case 3.

*Rain on the windshield. The car leaves the freeway at Fletcher Drive. On the left, between the houses, a long dark gap: the river. The bridge's old lamp posts march across it, and halfway along, the blue and red of a patrol car turn slowly in the rain.*

RAY (V.O.): The Los Angeles River. Most people in this town don't know it's there. The ones who do mostly live under it.

*Ray's phone buzzes on the dashboard. He reads it at the light.*

SHAH (text): fletcher bridge. east end. bring boots

SHAH (text): the uniforms are very pleased with themselves

RAY (V.O.): A kid on a bike, a man in a tent, and a patrol cop who wants to be home by five. Somebody's going to be disappointed.

*Cut to the bridge.*

### Scene 2. Fletcher Drive bridge (4:00 a.m.)

**Goal:** meet Doss and Preacher, read the bridge, and learn where its gutters go. Introduces the case's first gate: Preacher in cuffs.

*EXT. FLETCHER DRIVE BRIDGE. NIGHT. RAIN.*

*A 1927 concrete bridge over the river channel, deco railings, tall lamp posts with globes, half of them out. A painted bike lane along the curb. OFFICER BRIAN DOSS leans on his patrol car mid-span, lights turning, a door-mounted spotlight pointed uselessly at the road. In the back seat, behind the cage, PREACHER sits upright with his eyes closed. At the east end the railing gives way to a chain-link fence with a gap in it where the bridge meets the bank; beside it, concrete stairs lead down into the channel. Ray's car is parked at the west end.*

RAY: Fletcher Drive. Built in 1927, back when the city still thought the river was worth looking at.

DOSS: Detective! Brian Doss, Northeast. Sorry to drag you out. Honestly, it's a wrap. Kid's bike, guy's tent. Coroner's just being thorough.

RAY: She usually is.

**Talking to Doss.**

[choice] What have you got? / Who found him? / Why's he in your car? / Uncuff him. (needs evidence, see Puzzle 2.3) / Put your spotlight on the reeds. (after clue\_scupper or clue\_reeds\_tip) / That'll do.

*What have you got?*

DOSS: Owen Tate, twenty-four, rides for Chomp. He's down on the concrete under the east end. His e-bike was parked at that guy's tent. Two thousand bucks of bike. Guy says he "found it."

RAY: Maybe he did.

DOSS: They always found it.

RAY: Who's they?

DOSS: *(cheerfully)* You know. Them.

*Who found him?*

DOSS: Metro bus driver on the owl route, three twenty-two. Saw a yellow bag down in the channel in his headlights and called it in. Good eyes. I'd have missed it.

RAY: That's what worries me, Officer.

*Why's he in your car?*

DOSS: Calvin Odom, they call him Preacher. Army guy, lives under the bridge. Won't say a word to me. Says he'll only talk to "somebody who's been somewhere." *(beat)* I've been to Rancho Cucamonga.

*That'll do.*

DOSS: I'll be right here. My shift ends at five, so, you know. No rush.

**Talking to Preacher** (through the open back door). The first time:

*Preacher sits with his hands cuffed behind him, gray beard, Army field jacket, lips moving.*

PREACHER: "...and the waters returned from off the earth continually." Genesis, eight. You're the detective.

RAY: Ray Kessler.

PREACHER: That young man says I killed a boy for a bicycle. I told him I'd speak to somebody who's been somewhere.

**Puzzle 2.1: Somebody who's been somewhere.** A dialogue puzzle. Wrong answers just loop back, no penalty.

[choice] I've been somewhere. / Did you kill him? / Never mind.

*Did you kill him?*

PREACHER: The Lord knows I did not. That young man doesn't. Ask me better.

*I've been somewhere.*

PREACHER: Where?

[choice] Baghdad, oh-three. Military Police. / Hollywood Division, twenty-six years. / Everywhere, friend.

*Hollywood Division:* PREACHER: "That's a long time somewhere. It ain't what I asked."

*Everywhere, friend:* PREACHER: "A man who's been everywhere has been nowhere twice."

*Baghdad:* PREACHER: "MP. Then you know your motto, MP."

[choice] Assist, Protect, Defend. / Semper Fidelis. / Rangers lead the way. / To protect and to serve.

*Semper Fidelis:* PREACHER: "That's a Marine. You don't stand like a Marine." RAY (V.O.): "Twenty years. They take the motto back with the uniform."

*Rangers lead the way:* PREACHER: "You ain't no Ranger. Rangers don't drink that much coffee."

*To protect and to serve:* PREACHER: "That's painted on your car door. I asked about the Army."

*Assist, Protect, Defend:*

*Preacher opens his eyes.*

PREACHER: Assist, protect, defend. First Cavalry, Desert Storm. Seventy-two hours to Kuwait City and thirty years to get here. Calvin Odom. They call me Preacher because I read to the river.

RAY: Does it listen?

PREACHER: Better than most. *(sets preacher\_trusts)*

After preacher\_trusts, Preacher's menu:

[choice] What happened tonight? / Did you hear anything? / Did you see the boy? / Why carry the bike all the way down? / That'll do.

*What happened tonight?*

PREACHER: I was in my tent with Deuteronomy. Some while after two, I see a red light blinking on the water. Off, on, off, on. Up on the bridge. So I went up the stairs. A bicycle, leaning on the rail, light still going, nobody with it. Big yellow box on the back, all stove in.

PREACHER: I carried it down. By sunup the strippers would've had the wheels, the battery, the seat. I meant to hand it to the first police I saw. *(beat)* The first police I saw put me in this car. *(sets clue\_preacher\_story)*

*Did you hear anything?*

PREACHER: Two o'clock, about. A bang up top, like a dumpster lid. No brakes before it. I know brakes. Then quiet. Then a long while after, a car door. Then a car going away slow, like it was ashamed of itself.

RAY: How long a while?

PREACHER: Long enough for a man to do something. Not long enough to do it right. *(sets clue\_bang)*

*Did you see the boy?*

PREACHER: No, sir. He was on the far side, under the east end, in the dark. If I'd seen him I'd have gone to him. *(beat)* I'd have sat with him.

*Why carry the bike all the way down?*

PREACHER: Because nobody steals from my camp. That's the rule down there. I'm the rule.

*That'll do.*

PREACHER: I'll be in this car. Unless somebody decides different.

**Puzzle 2.2: The bridge remembers.** Three observations, any order; the storm drain is the one that matters.

Look at the road at the east end: "Wet asphalt, a painted bike lane, and no skid marks. Not one. Whoever hit him never touched the brakes." *(sets clue\_no\_brakes)*

Look at the gutter: "Crumbs of clear plastic in the gutter by the bike lane. Not windshield glass; that breaks into little cubes. This has edges. Headlight." *(sets clue\_glass\_bridge)*

Use the storm drain grate: "A storm drain, gurgling. Plastic glitter caught in the bars. The rain washed the big pieces down." Ray looks over the rail: "Bridges drain straight into the river. Whatever went through that grate came out under the bridge." *(sets clue\_scupper)*

**Puzzle 2.3: Uncuff him.** The Doss option "Uncuff him." works once the player has clue\_hit\_by\_car (Shah) and either clue\_two\_tracks or clue\_phone\_left (both in the channel). Before that:

DOSS: On what, a feeling?

RAY: I'll come back with more than a feeling.

With the evidence:

RAY: Shah says a car hit that kid from behind, hard enough to break both his legs at bumper height. Your man doesn't have a car.

RAY: *(with clue\_two\_tracks)* And the bike came down the stairs on one wheel. Owen came down the bank on his heels, dragged. Two trips, two different people. *(or, with clue\_phone\_left)* And a man who steals a two-thousand-dollar bike leaves a nine-hundred-dollar phone on the handlebars?

*Doss looks at the back seat for a long moment.*

DOSS: Northeast is gonna love this.

*He opens the door and unlocks the cuffs. Preacher steps out, rubs his wrists, looks at Ray, and says nothing. He walks to the stairs and goes down into the channel, back straight.* *(sets preacher\_freed; Preacher now appears at his tent in Scene 3)*

**Puzzle 2.4: The spotlight.** Using the patrol car's spotlight directly: DOSS: "Hands off the unit, Detective. City property." Once the player knows where to look (clue\_scupper or clue\_reeds\_tip), the Doss option "Put your spotlight on the reeds." appears:

RAY: Swing your spotlight over the rail. Under the bridge, on the reeds below the drain pipe.

DOSS: You want me to light up the river? At four in the morning? *(shrugs)* Sure. Not my electricity.

*He leans into the patrol car and swings the spotlight over the railing. A hard white beam drops into the channel and lands on a black island of reeds.* *(sets channel\_lit)*

**Hints.** If the player has clue\_hit\_by\_car but Preacher is still in the car: "Shah says a car. Doss says a tent. Only one of them went to medical school." If the reeds are dark and the player has clue\_scupper: "Everything that went down that drain is in the reeds. Doss has a spotlight he's very proud of."
