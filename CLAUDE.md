# Notes for AI agents working on Nightshift

- Engine: Godot 4.7 with GDScript. Pure Godot, no plugins. The base resolution is 1920×1080
  (canvas_items stretch, fractional scale, linear filtering), and all coordinates are in those pixels.
- Global state lives only in the `Game` autoload (`autoload/game.gd`). Anything that must survive a
  room change or a save is a flag (`Game.set_flag` / `Game.flag`) or an inventory item.
- Room logic goes in `scripts/rooms/<room>.gd`, which `extends Room` and overrides
  `interact(hs: Hotspot, verb: String, item: String)`. `verb` is `"look"` or `"use"`, and `item` is
  the inventory item id when one is used on the hotspot. These functions are coroutines, so always
  `await` the `main.*` calls.
- New items need an entry in `Game.ITEMS` and an 84×84 icon at `assets/items/<id>.png`
  (add the prop to `tools/r3/gen_icons.py`).
  Examine text is in `ITEMS[id].desc`, or add a case to `main.gd` `examine_item()`.
- Hotspots are `Area2D` nodes using `scripts/hotspot.gd`, each with a `CollisionPolygon2D` child.
  Their coordinates are room pixels (0..1920, 0..1080). `walk_to = Vector2.ZERO` means "don't walk",
  which suits look-only things far away.
- The walkable area is the `WalkArea` Polygon2D, minus any `Obstacles/*` Polygon2D footprints. Every
  `walk_to` and spawn point must be inside it and outside the obstacles.
- Walk-behind props (foreground occluders) are Sprite2D nodes under the y-sorted `Actors` node. Their
  `position` is the floor baseline and their `offset` places the image. They come from `occluders` in the set's
  `meta` (see the README). Don't fake depth with `z_index`.
- The detective is relit at runtime (`shaders/relight.gdshader`, driven by `Room.light_at()` from
  `assets/rooms/<room>_light.json`). Don't tint him with `modulate`. To change how he looks in a room,
  change the set's lights or `char_fill` and re-bake with `tools/r3/light_probes.py <room>`.
  `detective.png` and `detective_light.png` must keep the same frame layout.
- When typing results of dynamic calls, use explicit types (`var c: int = await main.choose(...)`),
  not `:=`, because `main` is untyped in room scripts.
- Art is generated, not hand-painted. Rooms and the detective come from 3D sets in `tools/r3/`
  (see the README). Change the set or the generator rather than editing PNGs by hand, then re-run
  `render_room.py`, `gen_sprites.py` and `build_scenes.py`.
- `scenes/rooms/*.tscn` are generated by `tools/r3/build_scenes.py`, so manual edits there get
  overwritten. Room behaviour belongs in `scripts/rooms/*.gd`.
